Red Autumn Social Democracy

Created by SylvurrTV & originn0

A mod which adds a sandbox menu to Dynamic Social Democracy: An Alternate History.

Play Github

Description

This mod adds a sandbox menu which enables you to play the Dynamic submod for Social Democracy: An Alternate History any way you want! It adds options such as modifying the budget & resources, clearing your hand and changing party support.

Note that mods do not work in the Steam version of the game.
