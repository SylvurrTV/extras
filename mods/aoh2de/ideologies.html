Age of History II: Definitive Edition

Created by SylvurrTV

A mod which adds new ideologies to AOH2:DE.

Steam Workshop

Description

This mod adds several new forms of government to AOH2:DE, for roleplay purposes! It can also be used to add more ideologies to scenarios you post on the workshop.

Note that there is a strange issue where tribes who reform into civilised nations will become socialist T-T


Ideologies

  • Social Democracy
  • Conservative Democracy
  • Liberal Democracy
  • Green Democracy
  • Democratic Socialism
  • Conservative Authoritarian Democracy
  • Socialist Authoritarian Democracy
  • Socialism
  • Anarchism
  • Military Junta
  • Corporatism
