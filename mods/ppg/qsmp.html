The QSMP Mod

People Playground

Created by SylvurrTV

A mod which adds players, characters, and items from the QSMP to People Playground.

Steam Workshop (Disabled) Github

Description

This mod includes most players from QSMP 1, as well as the eggs, several items and most non-creator characters, such as Cucurucho and The Code.

Thanks to the QSMP Wiki team for making it so much easier to do this...
