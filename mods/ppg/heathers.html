A mod which adds characters from Heathers: The Musical to People Playground.

Description

This mod includes every character from the 2014 Off-Broadway production of Heathers: The Musical, and a couple of props too!

Hopefully I get the chance to make more musical mods in the future, once modding is fixed in People Playground. Beetlejuice??
