Vespera Europa (Preview)

Simulation / Grand Strategy

Created by SylvurrTV

A modern-day grand strategy set in Europe.

Itch.io Github Play Preview

Description

This preview of Vespera Europa (sequel to Modernia 1) contains the full map of Europe, and a display of each province's name.


In the future, this game could follow a variety of paths, but currently I plan to include:

  • Policies and governments (including dynamic coalitions and decisions)
  • Intergovernmental organisations such as the EU and UN (NATO may not be included to the extent that exists in real life, but we'll see)
  • Espionage and sabotage
  • Diplomatic crises and solutions rather than war, although war will still be possible
  • Research and special optional branches of gameplay that provide more customisation and accomplish specific goals (eg space programmes)
  • Full scenario editor WITH event support (similar to Age of History II)
  • Several starting scenarios, although expect to only see 2026 for the foreseeable future
  • Mod / custom scenario loader
  • Sandbox mode
  • And more!

If you have any ideas or suggestions for future aspects of the game (or any provinces that could have better names) please leave a comment in the discussion on itch.io!
